\subsection{Requirements} \label{E.1}

Members may achieve Prestigious Active status by completing
at minimum the following requirements in the course of one term:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \subsecitem{a}{DA Status}
    Achieving Distinguished Active status as defined in Appendix \ref{D}.
    \item \subsecitem{b}{Total Involvement}
    Completing 32 total hours (including those from Distinguished
Actives Requirements), defined as follows:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Service hours and social or professional development credits count as 1 hour
        \item No more than 15 hours can be counted from a single event
        \item Only 8 total social or professional development credits can be counted
        \item Hours must come from at least 3 different service events.
    \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}


\subsection{Gift} \label{E.2}

At the end of each term, in appreciation for their significant contribution,
each Prestigious Active will be given a fitting reward to be determined at the
discretion of the Officer Corps.

\subsection{Timing and Deadlines} \label{E.3}

Any requirements completed following initiation
but before the end of term may be counted toward the current term or the following
full term. In addition, any requirements completed between the winter and fall terms may
be counted toward fall.


\subsection{Attaining PA during Initiation Semester} \label{E.4}

An electee may count any
hours beyond those needed for initiating toward achieving PA status. Electee PA requirements
are the same as those for an active except that the total number of hours required
is 33 (34 if no leadership credit is earned). Additionally, up to 10 social credits may be
counted toward this number.


\subsection{Exceptions} \label{E.5}

Where circumstances merit, the Membership Officer may
choose to allow any member to substitute service hours for any form of requirement, with
the exception of leadership credit, provided that the total number of hours completed is not
diminished as a result of this. With consent of the Executive Committee, the Membership
Officer may assign leadership credit to someone who has not otherwise earned it.
