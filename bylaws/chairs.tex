\subsection{Current Chair Positions} \label{H.1}

The Chairs and their descriptions follow:
\begin{enumerate}

    \item \subsecitem{a}{Website Chair}
    The Website Chair(s) shall be responsible for the management of the
Chapter’s website, including coordinating the development of new capabilities and
documenting the process for members to contribute to the website’s development.
This chair(s) serves on the Chapter Team and will report to the Chapter Development
Officer.

    \item \subsecitem{b}{Outreach Chair}
    The Outreach Chair is responsible for assisting the campus outreach
officer in the outreach activities and events of the chapter. This chair serves on
the Events Team and reports to the Campus Outreach Officer.

    \item \subsecitem{c}{Apparel Chair}
    The Apparel Chair(s) will be responsible for the design and acquisition of
any MI-G branded apparel that the chapter may desire. The chair(s) will serve on the
Chapter Team and reports to the Membership Officer.

    \item \subsecitem{d}{Alumni Relations Chair}
    The Alumni Relations Chair is responsible for developing
and maintaining the chapter’s alumni relations initiatives. The chair will work
with the Secretary and membership officer to maintain an up-to-date MI-G alumni
contact roster. This chair serves on the Chapter Team and reports to the Historian.

    \item \subsecitem{e}{Graduate Student Activities Chair}
    The Graduate Student Activities Chair(s)
will work with and report to the Graduate Student Vice President to help plan and
execute events for graduate student actives and candidates. Further, the chair(s) will
support the graduate student vice president in the execution of the latter’s duties.
The chair(s) will be members of the Events Team but shall collaborate with the Graduate
Student Vice President.

    \item \subsecitem{f}{Banquet Arrangements Chair}
    The Banquet Arrangements Chair will work with
the activities officer, the service coordinator, and the executive team to plan and execute
the chapter’s semesterly end of term banquet. This chair serves on the Events
team and will report to the Activities Officer.

    \item \subsecitem{g}{Mindset Chair}
    The Mindset Chair(s) will work with the K-12 Officers and Service
Coordinator to plan and execute MindSET events. The chair(s) will be members of
the Events Team and will report to the K-12 Officers.

    \item \subsecitem{h}{New Initiatives Chair}
    The New Initiatives Chair will assist with coordinating the
Chapter’s New Initiatives meetings, including identifying topics, chairing the meeting,
obtaining food, and summarizing discussion. The chair will work under the
direction of the Chapter Development Officer. The chair will be a member of the
chapter team and will report to the Chapter Development Officer.

    \item \subsecitem{i}{Merit Badge Day Chair}
    The Merit Badge Day Chair(s) will work with the K-12
Officers and Service Coordinator to plan and execute merit badge day events for boy
scouts. The chair(s) will be members of the Events Team and will report to the K-12
Officers.

    \item \subsecitem{j}{Career Fair Chair}
    The Career Fair Chair(s) will work under the direction of and
report to the External Vice Presidents to plan and execute the Fall Engineering Career
Fair. The chair(s) will undertake such tasks as the External Vice Presidents delegate
to them.

    \item \subsecitem{k}{Cub Scouts Day Chair}
    The Cub Scouts Day Chair(s) will organize, plan, and execute
at least one Cub Scouts Day event each academic semester. The chair(s) will be
members of the events team and will report to the K-12 Officers.

    \item \subsecitem{l}{Engineering Day for Detroit Chair}
    The Engineering Day for Detroit Chair(s)
will organize, plan, and execute Engineering Day events. The chair(s) will be members
of the Events team and will report to the K-12 Officers.

    \item \subsecitem{m}{NEST Project Manager Chair}

This chair serves on the Events Team and will report to the Service Coordinator.
This chair position will expire at the end of the Fall 2026 semester.

\end{enumerate}
