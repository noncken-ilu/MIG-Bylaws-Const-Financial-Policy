\subsection{Election Meeting, Quorum, Eligibility, and Nominations} \label{III.1}

Election
of advisors will take place at the officer election meeting, under the same quorum,
active status, and nomination procedures as described in Bylaw \ref{II}. Anyone meeting the
criteria in Bylaw \ref{I.2} is eligible to run for an advisor position.


\subsection{Candidate Speeches} \label{III.2}

Each advisor nominee may give a speech to, and
answer questions from, the general assembly. All nominees are limited to 1.5 minutes for
both speech and questions and answers. These limits may be extended by motion from the
floor.


\subsection{Candidate Discussion} \label{III.3}

Following each speech, the general assembly
will engage in discussion concerning the candidate. Since each advisor is treated as its
own office, in the event that multiple advisors pursue election in the same term, it is not
necessary that anyone but the candidate in question leave the room for discussion. This
discussion must remain strictly confidential and is limited to matters pertaining to an individual’s
ability to successfully carry out the responsibilities of the office.


\subsection{Winning the Election} \label{III.4}

Each candidate that receives a majority of the
votes cast (not counting abstentions as a vote for or against) will be elected to office. The
votes must be counted by three individuals who must be either advisors or officers not
pursuing election that semester.


\subsection{Term Length} \label{III.5}

Advisor term length will be subject to motion from the
floor as part of the election proceedings. An advisor candidate must indicate their preferred
term length, and cannot be elected to a term longer than that. The maximum length
for an individual term is three years. The term length is considered part of the motion to
elect and therefore may be amended by the body. If an advisor fails to receive a majority
of votes cast, a motion may be made to elect them to a shorter term, to a minimum of one
semester.


\subsection{Term Commencement} \label{III.6}

Advisor terms begin on the day following the
chapter’s main initiation directly following their election.
