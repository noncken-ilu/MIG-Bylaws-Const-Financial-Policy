\subsection{Appointed Offices} \label{IV.1}

The following officers will be appointed by the Officer
Corps: the External Vice Presidents (EVPs), both K-12 Outreach Officers, and any ad
hoc officers specified in Appendix \ref{F.4}.


\subsection{Default Appointment Procedure} \label{IV.2}

Unless otherwise specified, the
procedure for filling appointed officer positions is:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item An application will be made available at least 2 days prior to the officer election
meeting.
    \item The content of application will be decided upon by the current president and, where
applicable, the current holder(s) of the office in question.
    \item The application will remain open for a minimum of one week from the date it is provided
to the membership, but will not close less than 3 days after the officer election
meeting.
    \item Following the close of the application, interviews may be held for some or all of the
applicants at the discretion of the reviewing committee. If needed, at most three interviewers
will be determined by the reviewing committee. The default interviewers
will be the current president, the current lead for the Team in question and, where
applicable, the current holder of the office in question.
    \item The reviewing committee will consist of: \label{IV.2.e}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item The current Executive Committee,
        \item The incoming President,
        \item The current lead for the Team in question,
        \item The current holder of the office in question, and
        \item Any active member deemed necessary by the above groups.
    \end{enumerate}
    The current president will chair the reviewing committee and may not vote, except
in order to break a tie. The incoming president may not vote. In addition, active
members may request participation in the reviewing committee, to be approved by
the Executive Committee.
    \item A person applying for an office may not participate as an interviewer or part of the
reviewing committee for that office. \label{IV.2.f}
    \item The reviewing committee will meet to consider the received applications and, if applicable,
interview results. They will select the best candidate(s) by means of a majority
vote.
\end{enumerate}


\subsection{External Vice President Appointment} \label{IV.3}

The External Vice Presidents
(EVPs) will be appointed in the standard procedure, with the following modifications:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The application will be made available at least three weeks prior to the officer election
meeting.
    \item The application will remain open for at least two weeks.
    \item Interviews must be conducted. The interviewers will be the current president and
EVPs. In the event any interviewer is unable to attend an interview, or is himself/
herself an applicant, a substitute will be determined by the interviewers.
    \item The reviewing committee will be the current President, Vice President, Secretary, and
both EVPs. In the event that any members of this committee apply for EVP, they will
be recused from the decision. The first vacancy will be filled by the Treasurer, unless
they are also an applicant for EVP. Any remaining vacancies will be filled by advisors.
    \item The reviewing committee will decide the two most suitable candidates between the
conclusion of the interviews and the start of the officer elections meeting.
\end{enumerate}


\subsection{K-12 Outreach Officer Appointment} \label{IV.4}

Both K-12 Outreach Officers
will be appointed in the standard procedure, with the following modifications:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item If needed, the reviewing committee will appoint an interviewing committee, consisting
of at most three of the following:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item The outgoing and incoming Presidents,
        \item The outgoing and incoming Events Team Leads, and
        \item The outgoing and continuing K-12 Outreach Officers.
    \end{enumerate}
    The interviewing committee must include either the outgoing Events Team Lead or
the continuing K-12 Outreach Officer, without exclusion of either officer.
    \item The reviewing committee will consist of voting and non-voting members. The voting
members will be:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item The outgoing and incoming Events Team Leads, and
        \item The outgoing and continuing K-12 Outreach Officers, and
        \item Any active member deemed necessary by the above groups.
    \end{enumerate}
    The chair will be the continuing K-12 Outreach Officer, or be appointed by majority
vote if the continuing K-12 Outreach Officer is ineligible or unable to attend. As per
parliamentary procedure established by C§I.3, the chair may only vote after all other
votes have been cast.
    \item The non-voting members of the reviewing committee will be:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item All K-12 Outreach Committee members,
        \item The incoming President, and
        \item The outgoing President (other than to break a tie).
    \end{enumerate}
    \item Active members may request to participate in the reviewing committee as per \S \hyperref[IV.2.e]{IV.2.e}.
    \item The membership of both the interviewing and reviewing committees remains subject
to the prohibition on participation by applicants as per \S \hyperref[IV.2.f]{IV.2.f}.
\end{enumerate}


\subsection{Ad Hoc Officer Appointment} \label{IV.5}

Any ad hoc officers will be appointed
in the standard procedure, with the following exceptions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The application may be made available whenever is deemed most appropriate by the
officer corps.
    \item The application may close whenever is deemed most appropriate by the officer corps,
provided that it has remained open for a minimum of one week from the date it was
provided to the membership.
\end{enumerate}


\subsection{Chair Appointment} \label{IV.6}

The procedure for appointing Chair Positions is:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item An application will be made available whenever is deemed most appropriate by the
officer corps.
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item The content of the application will be decided upon by the Reporting Officer(s)
and, where applicable, the current holder(s) of the office in question.
        \item The application may close whenever is deemed most appropriate by the officer
corps, provided that it has remained open for a minimum of one week from the
date it was provided to membership.
    \end{enumerate}
    \item The Reporting Officer(s) of a Chair shall review applications and select the best candidate(
s) by means of a majority vote.
    \item The Reporting Officer(s) will notify the Officer Corps of the appointment(s) of Chairs
within one week. Additionally, the Reporting Officer(s) must notify non-selected
applicants within one week, including the rationale of their decision.
    \item The Officer Corps may, if requested by a non-selected applicant or on its own initiative,
review a non-selection, and reverse it by a majority vote.
\end{enumerate}


\subsection{Project Leaders} \label{IV.7}

Project Leaders are in charge of the planning and execution
of specific projects or events, including development of new events. Project Leaders
will be members of the Events Team, even though their work may be overseen by or fall
within the purview of Officer(s) beyond the Events Team.
