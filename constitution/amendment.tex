\subsection{Proposal} \label{VI.1}
Amendments to the Constitution may be proposed by any active member of the chapter.

\subsection{Notice} \label{VI.2}
Amendments to the Constitution must be presented to the membership at least 3 business days before the meeting at which a vote is to occur.
Amendments may be amended during the meeting without violating this requirement.
In the event that the vote is to take place at a meeting other than a regularly scheduled chapter voting meeting, notice of at least 2 weeks must be provided for the meeting.

\subsection{Adoption} \label{VI.3}
Any proposed amendment may be adopted by the approval of at least three fourths of those present and entitled to vote at a chapter voting meeting and by an affirmative vote of $\sfrac{5}{7}$ of the Advisory Board.
Such votes must occur within 14 days of each other.
