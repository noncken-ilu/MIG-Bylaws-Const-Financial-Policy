\subsection{Ratification}
This Constitution was proposed by the Michigan Gamma Advisory Board with the advice of the Officer Corps and was enacted 12 November, 2013 by ratification of the active membership of Michigan Gamma and of the chapter Advisory Board.

\subsection{Review}
This Constitution will be re-approved every five years, on years ending in 0 or 5, by a majority vote of the advisory board, regardless of changes.
