
\subsection{Policy} \label{IV.1}
All expenditures must be made in line with all relevant University financial policies and procedures.

\subsection{Method} \label{IV.2}
Individuals with budget authority and an approved budget may purchase items authorized under Policy \ref{III} for use in Chapter-sponsored activities. 
Such purchases must be made using a traceable method, such as with a credit card or purchase order. 
Purchases may be delegated to other individuals under the direction of an individual with budget authority. 
Expenditures for which a member seeks reimbursement are also subject to Policy \ref{VI}.

\subsection{Inventory Management} \label{IV.3}
Unused or reusable items purchased with Chapter funds should be added to the Chapter property inventory as defined in Policy \ref{V}. 
Exceptions to this requirement may be granted in writing by the Treasurer and President with notice sent to the Advisory Board.
